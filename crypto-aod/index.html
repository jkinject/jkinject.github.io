For Galaxy phones

Coin prices on your lock screen and AOD.

Check Bitcoin, Ethereum and more with a glance, even with the screen off. No unlocking, no opening an app.

How it works

  1. 1

    Add coins: pick one of 7 exchanges and search by name or symbol.

  2. 2

    Put a widget on the lock screen and Always On Display (Settings → Lock screen and AOD → Edit lock screen → Widgets) or on the home screen.

  3. 3

    Choose how often prices refresh: every 1, 5 or 15 minutes. Tap a widget to refresh right away.

Lock screen and AOD widgets need a Galaxy phone with One UI 7 or later. On other Android phones only the home screen widget works.

Always On Display & lock screen

  • 1×1 and 2×1 widgets, one coin each
  • Keeps refreshing while the screen is off
  • Shows the last price and time if a refresh fails

Home screen widget

  • Up to 5 coins as a list: symbol, price, daily change
  • Themes: Classic dark, Light, Glass, Big text, Minimal
  • Tap to refresh

7 exchanges

  • Upbit, Bithumb, Binance, Coinbase, OKX, Bybit, Kraken
  • Filter by quote currency: USDT, USD, EUR, KRW, BTC and more
  • Mix exchanges in one watchlist

Private by design

  • No account, no login
  • No ads, no analytics
  • Your watchlist stays on your phone
